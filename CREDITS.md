# Credits

## Inspiration

GenoCode is inspired by **DancerDNA** (Cyberation, published by Notting Hill Publishing, 1999), a
program that evolved creatures which danced to music. Its creature language, gene ranges,
breeding and morphing, and music-driven dance were the starting point for GenoCode. No DancerDNA
files or code are included in this project. The starter creatures and dance styles that were
inspired by DancerDNA's are rewritten from scratch in GenoCode, and each one names the original it
was inspired by.

GenoCode also grows out of **Fantastic Avian Creatures**, an earlier project by GenoCode's author.

## Other influences

- William Latham and Stephen Todd, FormGrow and Mutator: forms built from a fixed grammar whose
  parameters are genes, and animation by interpolating genes.
- Karl Sims, _Genetic Images_ and _Galápagos_: genomes that can grow.
- Scott Draves, _Electric Sheep_: an open-source, music-reactive, community-driven evolutionary
  artwork.
- Steven Rooke: seeding new work with genomes that have already been evolved.
- Matthew Lewis, _Evolutionary Visual Art and Design_: practical techniques for evolutionary
  design tools.
- Mitchell Whitelaw, _Metacreation: Art and Artificial Life_ (MIT Press, 2004), and the works it
  discusses, including Mutagen, Life Spacies, TechnoSphere, Eden and Breed.
